# Launch-day evidence checklist

## Identity and facts

- [ ] Network and canonical mint independently checked.
- [ ] Program, supply, decimals, metadata, and image match the reviewed plan.
- [ ] Actual authorities match the public authority matrix.
- [ ] Team holdings, initial purchases, and fee arrangements disclosed.

## Transactions and market route

- [ ] Current official platform / SDK / pool instructions checked and dated.
- [ ] Exact wallet transaction inspected: programs, amounts, recipients, permissions.
- [ ] Fees, quote asset, slippage limits, and funding requirement checked.
- [ ] Platform migration or self-managed liquidity route documented separately.
- [ ] Liquidity ownership/lock claim has mechanism-specific evidence.

## Operations

- [ ] Rehearsal record and remaining untested behavior published appropriately.
- [ ] Independent reviewer recorded, with actual evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Canonical links, incident contact, and monitoring health checks work.
- [ ] Public documents/screenshots checked for secrets and misleading claims.
- [ ] Launch receipt, disclosure snapshot, and budget ledger prepared.

Signing owner / independent reviewer / time:

A checked box or a passing offline script is not on-chain verification or launch authorization.
