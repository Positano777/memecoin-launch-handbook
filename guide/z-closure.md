# Z - Zero ambiguity when plans change

Plan for a paused roadmap, an unavailable maintainer, depleted funding, a failed integration, or an orderly shutdown. Explain what remains operational, which services will stop, and which on-chain states cannot be reversed. Do not imply that ending software maintenance redeems or cancels a publicly traded token.

Preserve source code, licenses, canonical addresses, launch receipts, and accounting records. Archive unsupported services with a visible notice and provide export/migration instructions where appropriate. Identify who can maintain domains and respond to impersonation after the team changes.

After a launch or an incident, compare what happened with the original plan. Publish corrections, retire misleading screenshots, and turn reproducible failures into tests or documentation. The useful output of an A-to-Z process is a record people can inspect, not just a token that exists.

**Deliverable:** [closure and handover checklist](../templates/closure.md) plus a short retrospective.
