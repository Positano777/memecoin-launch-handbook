# T - Treasury, fees, and contributor funding

Separate token allocations, creator trading fees, donations, pool revenue, and money actually received by the treasury. They are not interchangeable. A configured beneficiary is not a payment receipt; gross trading volume is not treasury income.

If creator fees support open-source work, publish recipients, control arrangements, claim/distribution steps, actual receipts, expenses, and reporting dates. Verify the current platform's fee-sharing and claim semantics rather than copying a third-party app's assumptions. Some optional creation settings change who receives fees.

Use a treasury process with clear proposal, approval, execution, and bookkeeping responsibilities. A multisig can make approvals visible but does not guarantee good spending. Resolve the license scope of any reused treasury SDK before copying code.

**Deliverable:** [treasury policy](../templates/treasury-policy.md) and [budget ledger](../templates/budget.csv).
