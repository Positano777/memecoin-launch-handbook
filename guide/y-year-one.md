# Y - Year-one maintenance

Treat maintenance as a recurring responsibility. Schedule dependency and license checks, source/IDL change reviews, metadata and domain checks, treasury reporting, and contributor triage. Date your documentation and flag unverified changes rather than letting instructions silently expire.

Keep versioned examples and test fixtures for critical paths. A successful creation does not establish that future fee claims, liquidity operations, or wallet integrations remain compatible. Read platform release notes before upgrading adapters.

Publish the actual maintenance budget and whether the project can continue without token-related income. Separate funded milestones from volunteer work. Review signers and permissions when people leave; test the operational effect before making irreversible authority changes.

**Deliverable:** [operations schedule](../templates/operations.md) and a public monthly update covering delivered work, expenses, incidents, and unresolved limitations.
