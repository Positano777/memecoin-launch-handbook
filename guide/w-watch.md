# W - Watch after launch

Define what you will monitor and who will respond. Useful signals include metadata changes, retained-authority changes, treasury movements, creator-associated outflows, pool/liquidity changes, broken links, and missing data. Establish baseline observations before interpreting a change.

Differentiate sales from transfers, burns, locks, and account rent refunds. Preserve transaction links and confidence limits in alerts. Do not turn a missing top-holder entry into proof of a sale, or a shared funder into proof of common ownership.

Test provider failure and notification delivery. A quiet feed could mean no event, an expired subscription, a rate limit, or a broken worker. Publish monitoring coverage and expected delays. Managed APIs may be useful, but open SDK source does not make hosted access free.
