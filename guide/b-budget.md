# B - Budget before transactions

Build an operating budget rather than a target market capitalization. Include network fees, account rent, metadata hosting, platform/pool charges, liquidity inventory if applicable, monitoring, domain renewals, and contributor compensation. Separate a recoverable deposit from an expense, and token inventory from SOL/USDC cash.

Record every quote with its date and source. A launch page advertising free creation does not mean that buying, account creation, priority fees, migration, or external interfaces cost nothing. Network fees and launch-platform fees are different categories; failed transactions may still cost network fees. Use a rehearsal to measure the transactions you actually intend to submit.

**Deliverable:** [a budget ledger](../templates/budget.csv) with funding owner and contingency. Do not prefill a universal minimum SOL balance or estimate trade profits.

Sources: [network fee model](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees), [current Pump fees](https://pump.fun/docs/fees).
