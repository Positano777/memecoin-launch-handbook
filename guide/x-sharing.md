# X - X posts and public communications

Lead with something verifiable: a released tool, an upstream contribution, a fixed false positive, a launch receipt, or an expense report. Explain the user's problem and show an actual labeled example. Do not present a mockup or synthetic fixture as a live result.

Credit upstream maintainers and disclose paid promotion or team holdings relevant to the post. Avoid promises of returns, fabricated partnerships, fake volume, undisclosed coordinated buying, or calling ordinary launch-slot wallets dishonest. A clear limitation can make a technical post more useful.

Prepare launch and incident messages before you need them. Include the canonical mint location, supported feature state, and source links. Keep private dashboards and credential-bearing URLs out of screenshots. Images should explain the workflow rather than imply a safety certification.

**Deliverable:** [announcement templates](../templates/announcements.md) with a technical proof link and a correction process.
